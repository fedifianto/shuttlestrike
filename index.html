SHUTTLE STRIKE

First-person badminton. Your hand is the racket.

  • GripClose your hand as if holding a racket handle ✊ — the racket sits in your fist and tilts with your wrist.
  • SwingSwing your closed hand fast when the shuttle reaches the racket head. Swing left or right to aim.
  • ShotsRacket high + hard swing = smash. Racket low = lift/clear. Soft swing = drop shot.
  • PauseOpen your palm ✋ and hold it still to pause. Close your hand again ✊ to resume or rematch.
  • FootworkAutomatic — you run to the shuttle. First to 11, win by 2.
